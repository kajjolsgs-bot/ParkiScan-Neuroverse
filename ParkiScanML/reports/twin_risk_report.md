# Digital twin — 2-hour freezing risk (synthetic cohort)

synthetic cohort: 400 patients × 7 days, 30-min samples while awake, day 0 burn-in.

Validation: patient-grouped 5-fold CV. Prevalence of a freeze within 2 h: 6.7%.

| inputs | ROC AUC | PR AUC | Brier |
|---|---|---|---|
| ehr_only | 0.779 | 0.255 | 0.0556 |
| wearable_only | 0.759 | 0.256 | 0.0557 |
| ehr+wearable | 0.799 | 0.277 | 0.0550 |
| digital_twin (ehr+wearable+pk) | 0.806 | 0.281 | 0.0547 |

Top-decile alerts capture 45% of the 2-h windows that contain a freeze (precision 30%, lift 4.5×).

**Read honestly:** labels are generated by the simulator in `parkiscan/twin/sim.py` (hazard parameters listed in the JSON report). The comparison shows that fusing static records, wearables and the levodopa model recovers more of the simulated risk than any single stream; real-world accuracy is unknown.
