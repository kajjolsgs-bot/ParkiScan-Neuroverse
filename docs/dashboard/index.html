      Intended use and limits
      
          
        
            
      • Research prototype for the Happiest Health Digital Twin challenge. Not a medical device; no output is a diagnosis or a treatment instruction.
        • 
            
      • Seconds-ahead freezing forecast: trained and evaluated leave-one-subject-out on 10 people (Daphnet, lab protocol that provokes freezing). Home performance will differ.
        • 
            
      • Hours-ahead twin risk: trained on a synthetic cohort whose labels come from a documented simulator. It shows the fusion pipeline works end to end; it is not clinical accuracy.
        • 
            
      • All patient records are synthetic (FHIR R4-shaped, tagged SYNTHETIC) or anonymised open data, as required by the DPDP Act sandbox rules.
