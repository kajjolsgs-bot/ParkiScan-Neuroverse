# ParkiScan Neuroverse · Game + Digital Twin — demo video script (≈ 22 minutes)

Record at 1920×1080. Screen + voice-over; a small face-cam bubble is fine (it shows the webcam tasks are real). Upload as an **unlisted YouTube** video and paste the link in the README.

**Before recording**
1. `cd ParkiScanML && ./run_local.sh` — keep the terminal visible for 5 seconds in section 6, then minimise.
2. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8765/dashboard` in a browser tab (optional — the same dashboard opens inside the game with F2).
3. Unity: open `Assets/ParkiScan/Scenes/Demo.unity`, Game view maximised, 1920×1080. Good, even front lighting on your face; sit ~60 cm from the laptop.
4. Close notifications. Have `docs/ParkiScan_Neuroverse_Game_DigitalTwin_Presentation.pdf` open for sections 1–2 and 9.

| # | time | section | on screen | what to say (key points) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–2:00 | **The problem** | slides 1–3 | Team and college. Parkinson's in India: ≈ 5.8 lakh people, ~10% of the world's burden. Freezing of gait: ~50% early, up to ~80% later, a leading cause of falls; happens at home, between clinic visits. Care today is reactive. Our goal: a twin that sees the freeze coming. |
| 2 | 2:00–4:00 | **What our digital twin is** | slides 4–5 + architecture PDF | One virtual patient = health record + wearable streams + a levodopa model + a home game. It updates continuously, simulates ON/OFF, predicts one adverse event (freezing of gait) seconds and hours ahead, and lets the clinician test what-ifs. Two evidence levels: real recordings (seconds ahead) vs synthetic cohort (hours ahead) — we never mix them. Sandbox rules: synthetic / anonymised data only, local-only. |

Mention: iris mesh, calibration error is measured and recorded. Memory Core voice tasks (aah, pa-ta-ka, clap rhythm). Recover the memory objects. |
| 4 | 6:30–8:30 | **Level 2 (hand, sequencing, reaction)** | Unity: Level 2 — trace the spiral, connect nodes, repair, reactor signal | Spiral = drawing tremor & smoothness proxy; nodes = sequencing; repair = planning; signal = reaction time while walking. Roadmap: pilot with a movement-disorders clinic in Bengaluru, ABDM/FHIR integration, home ankle sensor, clinical validation. Close: "the twin turns freezing from a surprise into a forecast." |
